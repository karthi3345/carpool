Answers to Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking rides, driver verification, payments, and safety on TamizhPayanam.

Q1

How does carpooling work on TamizhPayanam?

TamizhPayanam connects car owners driving between cities with passengers heading in the same direction. Drivers publish routes and seats; passengers book instantly and split fuel and toll costs affordably.

Q2

How are drivers and passengers verified?

Every driver must complete Government ID verification (Aadhaar / Driving License), mobile OTP authentication, and RC book checks. Passengers are profile-verified with ratings from prior trips.

Q3

How does payment work? Can I pay UPI?

We support secure payments via UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), cards, and net banking. Escrow holds your payment safely until the ride is completed smoothly.

Q4

What is the cancellation and refund policy?

If a driver cancels, you receive an immediate 100% full refund. Passenger cancellations made at least 24 hours prior to departure receive a 100% refund without questions.

Q5

What is the "Explore Along the Route" feature?

"வழியிலே சுற்றுலா" lets you discover famous temples, traditional roadside eateries, scenic viewpoints, and heritage landmarks along highways with estimated detour timings.

Q6

Is it safe for women travelling solo?

Yes! We provide dedicated "Women Only" ride filters, 24/7 SOS buttons, live GPS trip sharing with family members, and verified community profiles for 100% comfort.
