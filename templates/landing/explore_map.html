Brihadisvara Temple (Big Temple)

தஞ்சைப் பெருவுடையார் கோயில்

Standing tall for over 1,014 years, this monolithic granite marvel was commissioned by Emperor Raja Raja Chola I. Featuring an 80-tonne single granite capstone atop its 216-foot vimana, it remains the crowning jewel of Chola Dravidian architecture.
